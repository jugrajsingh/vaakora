Vaakora

Speak to type. Listen to read. All on your own computer.

Vaakora is local dictation, transcript cleanup and read aloud for Linux. Audio and text stay on the machine. It is a Linux desktop app, built and tested on Fedora 44 (KDE Plasma, Wayland).

Features

  • DictationPress a shortcut, speak, and your words appear in any text field.
  • CleanupA small language model can tidy the transcript, on your computer or with a cloud provider.
  • Read aloudAnother shortcut reads the text you copied back to you.
  • TablesTables are read row by row, with the column names.
  • VocabularyChange how words are spoken, for every voice or for one.
  • HistoryPast dictations and past reads, text only.
  • ModelsDownload and choose the models for dictation, read aloud and cleanup.
  • SettingsShortcuts, sound and the rest.
  • Command lineA vaakora command drives the running app, and optional agent skills read aloud on request.
  • Moving from HandyThe first start copies your Handy settings and data.
